# Roles and RACI

R = responsible for execution · A = accountable decision owner · C = consulted · I = informed. Populate names and 24/7 contacts for each organization.

| Activity | Incident lead | Analyst | Identity / IT | Mail admin | Endpoint | Business / finance | Legal / privacy | Executive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Open record; provisional severity | A | R | I | I | I | C | C | I |
| Validate message and campaign scope | A | R | C | R | C | C | I | I |
| Revoke sessions / reset identity | A | C | R | C | I | C | I | I |
| Purge mail / adjust controls | A | C | C | R | I | I | I | I |
| Isolate endpoint | A | C | C | I | R | C | I | I |
| Pause payment / verify supplier | C | I | I | I | I | A/R | C | I |
| Determine notification obligations | C | C | I | I | I | C | A/R | I |
| Approve external statement | C | I | I | I | I | C | C | A/R |
| Approve recovery and closure | A | R | R | R | R | C | C | I |
| Track corrective actions | A | R | R | R | R | R | C | I |

The incident commander coordinates; service owners execute in their systems. Legal/privacy and authorized leadership assess applicable notification decisions. Finance uses known-good contact details. Record role and required independent approvals when one person holds multiple roles. Escalation contacts and authority limits are organization-specific.
