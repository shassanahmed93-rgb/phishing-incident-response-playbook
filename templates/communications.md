# Communications Templates

Adapt with authorized owners and verified channels. These are not legal notification language.

## Affected user

**Subject:** Security review: please contact the response team

We are reviewing a message that may have affected your work account. Please do not click the link, reply, or delete the original. If you interacted, contact **[verified help desk route]** and tell us whether you opened a link/file, entered a password, approved MFA, replied, or shared information. There is no blame; this helps protect your account. We will contact you through an approved channel with next steps.
